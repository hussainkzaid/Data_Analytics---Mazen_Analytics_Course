# Data Analytics & Visualization Dashboards

A repository featuring interactive business intelligence dashboards and analytical projects focused on logistics, supply chain performance, and survey analysis.

---

## Portfolio Projects

### 1. Movies Dashboard (`/Moves_Dashboard`)
* **Overview:** An exploratory data analysis dashboard focused on the film and entertainment industry.
* **Key Features:**
  * Analyzes revenue trends, budget distributions, and genre performance over time.
  * Highlights top-performing directors, actors, and overall box office ROI.
  * Displays viewer rating trends and critical metrics across releases.

### 2. Supply Chain Dashboard (`/Supply_Chain_Dashboard`)
* **Overview:** A business intelligence dashboard designed to monitor end-to-end logistics operations and performance metrics.
* **Key Features:**
  * Tracks key performance indicators (KPIs) such as on-time delivery rates, fulfillment lead times, and order accuracy.
  * Evaluates inventory levels, stockout risks, and supplier reliability metrics.
  * Visualizes regional shipment distribution and operational bottlenecks.

### 3. Survey Dashboard (`/Survey_Dashboard`)
* **Overview:** A dashboard built for analyzing raw survey response data to derive actionable demographic and sentiment insights.
* **Key Features:**
  * Aggregates survey responses across target demographic segments.
  * Visualizes feedback distributions, satisfaction ratings, and Net Promoter Scores (NPS).
  * Interactive filtering by location, age group, or specific response criteria.
